📲 Share or Install StudyFlow on Your Son's Computer

No server or publishing required — this entire planner runs inside a single standalone file.

Option 1 (Easiest): Send Him the Standalone App File

Click below to download StudyFlow-Planner.html with your current assignments, working hours, and personal plans already baked in.

How your son uses it on his Mac, PC, or Chromebook:
  1. Email, AirDrop, text, or Google Drive StudyFlow-Planner.html to his computer.
  2. He saves it to his Desktop or Documents folder and double-clicks it — it opens immediately in Chrome, Safari, or Edge!
  3. He can press Cmd+D (Mac) or Ctrl+D (Windows/Chromebook) to bookmark it on his browser bar, and any changes he makes automatically save on his computer.
Option 2: Share or Update Weekly Plan Data (.json)

Since each computer saves its own data locally, you can also export a tiny .json plan file to send him new assignments (or have him send you his updated schedule).

Option 3 (Optional): Publish a Free Private Web Link

If you want a web URL he can open on his phone, tablet, or laptop without sending a file: drag and drop StudyFlow-Planner.html (renamed to index.html in a folder) onto app.netlify.com/drop or tiiny.host. In Chrome/Safari/Edge he can then click "Install App / Add to Dock".
